    How discipline and specialty are assigned

    The workbook has no Discipline column. The build reads Standard Title and Profession - Specialty and keeps the first group that matches.

    1. LPN/LVN — LPN, LVN, licensed practical nurse, or licensed vocational nurse.
    2. CNA — CNA, STNA, GNA, PCT, certified nursing assistant, nursing assistant, nurse aide, or patient care tech.
    3. RN — the standard title starts with RN, the text says RN or Registered Nurse, or the profession starts with Nursing.
    4. Allied — lab, imaging and radiology, therapy (PT, OT, SLP, respiratory), and surgical tech, plus pharmacy, dietetics, dental hygiene, sleep, EEG, medical physics, social work, and tumor registry. A profession marked Allied stays here.
    5. Other — no match. Non-clinical titles land here.

    The Specialty filter is the Standard Title, the normalized specialty from Chief of Jobs. A blank title falls back to the text after the last “|” or “ - ” in Profession - Specialty.
